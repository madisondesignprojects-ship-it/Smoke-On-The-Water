Our story

Named for the river (and the riff)

Smokes on the Water started in Beaver Dam in 2017. That November we opened shop number two on West Main Street in Stoughton, in a multi-level building on the Yahara River, and the name says it all: a nod to the classic Deep Purple song. Founder Chris Kelley wanted small towns with real demand, and Wisconsin delivered.

Horicon joined the family in 2022, and Oregon became shop number four in February 2025. The idea hasn’t changed: a welcoming, inclusive shop with friendly, knowledgeable staff, competitive prices, and a crew that helps you find exactly what you need, whether you’re a seasoned vaper or brand new.

FAQ

Do I need an ID?

Yes. You must be 21 or older, and we check ID for every purchase at every location.

Where are your stores?

Four Wisconsin shops: Beaver Dam (501 N Spring St), Stoughton (233 W Main St), Horicon (309 E Lake St) and Oregon (201 Janesville St). See all locations for maps and directions.

What are your hours?

All four shops are open every day, 10am–9pm.

Can you order something you don't carry?

Usually, yes. We're always expanding our inventory and are happy to bring in items on request. Just ask at the counter or call your local shop.

Do you ship online orders?

Yes. Shop online at our store, and orders over $100 ship free. See our Shipping & Returns policy for details.

What payment do you accept?

Credit and debit cards, plus tap-to-pay at our Stoughton shop. Call your local shop to confirm what it accepts.
