Our story

Named for the river (and the riff)

Smokes on the Water opened on West Main Street in November 2017, our second Wisconsin shop after Beaver Dam. Founder Chris Kelley wanted a small town with real demand, and the multi-level building on the Yahara River was the perfect fit. The name is a nod to the classic Deep Purple song.

Today we're a four-shop Wisconsin family (Stoughton, Beaver Dam, Horicon and Oregon), but the idea hasn't changed. We want a welcoming, inclusive shop with friendly, knowledgeable staff, competitive prices, and a crew that helps you find exactly what you need, whether you're a seasoned vaper or brand new.

Our two house rules: we check IDs, and we keep it legal.

Good to know

FAQ

Do I need an ID?

Yes. You must be 21 or older, and we check ID for every purchase.

What are your hours?

We're open every day, 10am–9pm, at all four locations.

Can you order something you don't carry?

Usually, yes. We're always expanding our inventory and are happy to bring in items on request. Just ask at the counter or call (608) 205-6868.

Do you ship online orders?

Yes. Shop online at our store, and orders over $100 ship free. See our Shipping & Returns policy for details.

What payment do you accept?

Credit cards, debit cards and NFC mobile payments (tap to pay).

Is the store accessible?

Our street-level entrance is wheelchair accessible.
